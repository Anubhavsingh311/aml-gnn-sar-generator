How to read this

Flagged is the balanced alert: on the test period of IBM HI-Small (synthetic, 5.08M transactions, 0.10% illicit) about 78% of flagged transfers were illicit (precision) and about 64% of all illicit transfers were caught (recall).

High-recall is the wider net: recall about 87%, but precision only about 10%, so roughly 9 in 10 alerts are false positives. Every flagged row is also high-recall. A row can be neither. Precision depends on how rare illicit activity is in your file.

Treat results as a triage queue for human review, not accusations. Scoring is retrospective (it uses transactions before and after each one), the model was trained on synthetic data only, and it is not for real compliance decisions.
